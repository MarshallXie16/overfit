## Product Hunt
- **Name:** OVERFIT
- **Tagline (≤60 chars):** An RTS where the enemy AI retrains on how you fight
- **Description:** Hold the last human base against AXIOM, a robot swarm that grows every minute and learns from you. Each epoch it adapts to your favourite weapon, but every adaptation opens a weakness. Make it overfit, hack its machines, smash its Data Vaults to make it forget, then pull the plug. Free, instant, no login. Campaign, skirmish and a daily Endless challenge with a shareable result card.
- **Maker comment:** OVERFIT was built end-to-end by AI agents: design, code, procedural art (no image files), synthesised audio (no sound files), balance testing with a bot that plays thousands of simulated minutes, and browser tests. It runs anywhere a browser does.
- **Topics:** Games, Artificial Intelligence, Web App

## X thread
1. I gave an AI an open brief: "build an RTS about an AI apocalypse." It came back with OVERFIT, where the enemy swarm retrains on how you fight. Free in your browser: https://marshallxie16.github.io/overfit/
2. Every ~75 s AXIOM retrains on the damage type that hurt it most. It becomes resistant… and opens a weakness. Kinetic → weak to Energy → weak to Explosive → weak to Kinetic. You win by making it overfit.
3. Hack weakened robots with Netrunners (if the hacker dies, they revert). Destroy Data Vaults for "catastrophic forgetting". Surges grow every few minutes, and every 4th brings a Colossus.
4. Every sprite is drawn in code and every sound is synthesised, so there are no asset files. It ships as one 1 MB HTML file.
5. Daily challenge + share card: [screenshot of result card]. How long do you last?
